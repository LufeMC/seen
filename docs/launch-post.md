# X launch post

“Where was that tweet I read yesterday?”

I built Seen to find it.

Press ⇧⌘Space. Ask what you remember. Open the saved screen.

Native Mac app. Automatic Jev search with your own key. Local fallback.

Open source. MIT.
https://github.com/LufeMC/seen

## Attachment

Use `docs/screenshots/seen-history.jpg`. It shows the app with fictional demo content.
A short recording of the same demo can show the shortcut, question, result, and preview.
The demo uses local search. Do not present it as a measured Jev latency test.

## Optional follow-up

Captures and OCR stay on your Mac. If you save a Jev key, searches send candidate text to TypeSafe or Vercel AI Gateway.
The default history limit is seven days or 5,000 captures. The source includes setup and privacy details.
